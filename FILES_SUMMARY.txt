🎯 PROMPT FORMAT - CRITICAL REQUIREMENT:

The tool ONLY works with this exact format:

    perform addition on two numbers X and Y

✅ WORKS:
    - perform addition on two numbers 5 and 3
    - perform addition on two numbers 10 and 20
    - perform addition on two numbers 100 and 200

❌ DOESN'T WORK:
    - What is 5 + 3?
    - Calculate 5 + 3
    - Add 5 and 3
    - What's 10 plus 20?

This requirement is documented in ALL 4 files at strategic locations:
  • README: Overview + Usage section
  • QUICKSTART: At the top (⚠️ WARNING)
  • CONFIGURATION: Early in the document
  • TESTING: In table of contents and each test case
